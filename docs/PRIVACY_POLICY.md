## 1. Introduction

Smeet (“**we**”, “**us**”, “**our**”) operates the Smeet mobile application and related services (together, the “**Services**”) from **Brisbane, Australia**. We are committed to protecting your privacy in line with the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) (including the Australian Privacy Principles, or “**APPs**”) and, where applicable, other Australian privacy laws.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights available to you.

By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

---

## 2. Information we collect

We collect information that you provide directly, information generated when you use the Services, and limited technical information from your device.

### 2.1 Account and authentication

- **Email and password** (if you register with email/password) processed through **Supabase** authentication. Passwords are handled using industry-standard practices on Supabase’s side (for example, hashing consistent with Supabase’s implementation; we do not store plaintext passwords on your device in our systems in a reversible form).
- **Google Sign-In** and **Apple Sign-In** identifiers and tokens as needed to create or link your account.

### 2.2 Profile and preferences

- Display **name**, **profile photo**, **city** or locality, **sports** you are interested in, **skill** level or similar ability indicators, other sport and activity preferences, bio or similar fields you choose to add, age bracket or eligibility-related information where collected, and other profile details you submit.

### 2.3 Location

- **Approximate or precise location** (depending on your device permissions and in-app settings) used to show nearby people, games, venues, and matching relevant to Brisbane and surrounding areas. You can usually disable location in your device settings; some features may not work without it.

### 2.4 Activity and social features

- **Swipes, likes, matches,** and similar interactions.
- **User-generated content (“UGC”)** such as posts, comments, messages, media uploads, and **battle reports** or competitive/social summaries you submit through the app.
- Metadata associated with that content (for example, timestamps).

### 2.5 Notifications

- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** device tokens so we can send push notifications you opt into. Tokens are not used to identify you by name outside our systems.

### 2.6 Diagnostics and reliability

- **Sentry** for error reporting and performance diagnostics. We configure Sentry to **avoid collecting unnecessary personally identifiable information (PII)** where practicable; however, incidental data (for example, device type, app version, coarse network or routing information, or limited contextual strings) may be included in crash reports. Do not paste highly sensitive information into free-text fields if you are concerned about diagnostic logging.

### 2.7 Technical and usage data

- IP address, user agent, app version, language, and similar data from infrastructure providers (for example **Vercel** for our web properties, **Supabase** for APIs) as needed to operate and secure the Services.

We do **not** knowingly collect sensitive information (for example, health records or government identifiers) unless you voluntarily include such information in UGC—in which case you should consider that content may be visible to others as described below.

## 3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- Create and maintain your account and authenticate you.
- Operate core features: discovery, matching, messaging, feed, battles/competitions, and venue-related experiences oriented to **sports social** use in **Brisbane and Australia**.
- Personalise content and suggestions (for example, by sport, proximity, or activity).
- Send service-related messages and (where you consent) marketing or engagement notifications via push.
- Maintain safety and integrity: detect abuse, enforce our [Terms of Service](https://smeet.com.au/terms-of-service.html), and investigate misuse.
- Improve reliability and performance, including through analytics and error monitoring.
- Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from public authorities.

We do **not** sell your personal information to data brokers or advertisers for their independent marketing purposes.

## 5. Data storage and security

- We use **HTTPS/TLS** for data in transit between the app and our services where supported.
- Access controls and **Row Level Security (RLS)** (via Supabase) help ensure users can only access data they are permitted to see, subject to correct configuration and your account security.
- Authentication providers apply industry practices (for example, **bcrypt** or equivalent password hashing through **Supabase**, depending on provider version and configuration).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials and devices.

## 6. Your rights (Australia)

Under the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and APPs, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you (subject to exceptions in law).
- **Correct** personal information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading.
- **Request deletion** of your account and associated data, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must retain).
- **Withdraw consent** where processing is based on consent (for example, certain notifications or optional features), without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
- **Lodge a complaint** with the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)** if you believe we have mishandled your personal information. You may contact the OAIC at [www.oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au) or 1300 363 992.

To exercise access, correction, or deletion, contact **[support@smeet.com.au](mailto:support@smeet.com.au)**. We will respond within a reasonable period required or permitted by law.

## 7. Data retention

- **Active accounts:** we retain information needed to provide the Services until you delete your account or we no longer need it for the purposes described in this Policy.
- **After account deletion:** we aim to delete or de-identify personal information within **30 days**, except where we must retain certain records for **legal, regulatory, tax, or dispute-resolution** purposes, fraud prevention, or backups that roll off on a defined cycle.

UGC you have shared with others may remain visible in cached copies, other users’ inboxes, or third-party systems outside our control for a period after deletion.
