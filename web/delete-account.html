How to delete your account

Effective date: 2026-05-19 (19 May 2026)  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-19 (19 May 2026)  ·  Brisbane, Australia

At a glance: Smeet lets you delete your account from the app (Profile → Legal → Request account deletion) or by email to support@smeet.com.au. We process requests within 30 days. Most personal data is removed; some anonymized analytics and legally required records may be kept as described below.

1. App and developer

This page applies to Smeet, a sports social app operated for users in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia and nearby communities. Developer / operator name: Smeet. Related policies: Privacy Policy · Terms of Service.

2. How to request account deletion

You can request deletion in either of the following ways. You must be signed in for the in-app method.

Option A — In the Smeet app (recommended)

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Legal & safety section.
  3. Tap Request account deletion (shown in red at the bottom of that section).
  4. Read the confirmation screen, then tap Continue.
  5. Type DELETE when prompted to confirm your intent.
  6. Submit the request. You will receive a reference number — keep it for your records. You will be signed out after the request is recorded.

Deletion is not always instant in the app; your request is queued for processing on our servers (see Section 5).

Option B — By email

Email support@smeet.com.au with:

  • Subject line: Account deletion request
  • The email address linked to your Smeet account
  • Your display name (if known) to help us locate your account

We may reply to verify your identity before processing the request.

3. What data is deleted

After we process your deletion request, we aim to remove or de-identify personal data associated with your account, including:

CategoryExamples
ProfileName, email, city, sports preferences, skill level, bio, profile photos
Feed contentYour posts, photos, videos, and comments on the Feed
ChatYour messages and participation in chats (see note below)
Social graphMatch history, likes, and swipe-related records tied to you
GamesYour game participation and hosting history where stored as personal data
AccountAuthentication credentials and app account record

Chat note: Messages you sent may still appear on another user’s device or in their chat history until they refresh or delete their copy. We remove your data from our systems as part of processing.

4. What data may be kept

Some information may be retained after deletion, including:

  • Anonymized or aggregated analytics that cannot reasonably be linked back to you;
  • Records required by law — for example tax, fraud prevention, or regulatory obligations — for up to 7 years where applicable;
  • Backup copies on rolling backups, which are purged as backups rotate (typically within 90 days).

We do not keep your data for marketing after a completed deletion, except as allowed by law.

5. Timeline

We aim to complete account deletion and removal of associated personal data within 30 days after we receive and verify your request, as stated in our Privacy Policy. Complex cases or legal holds may take longer; we will contact you at your account email if we need more time.
