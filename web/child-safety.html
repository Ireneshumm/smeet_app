Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 2026-05-19 (19 May 2026)  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-19 (19 May 2026)  ·  Brisbane, Australia

At a glance: Smeet has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Our app is for adults 18+ only. You can report and block users in the app. Child safety concerns: child-safety@smeet.com.au.

1. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Smeet (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates a sports social app for adults in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia and nearby communities. We have zero tolerance for:

  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form;
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — including creating, sharing, soliciting, or possessing such material on or through our Services;
  • Any conduct that sexualises, grooms, or endangers children.

Accounts involved in CSAE or CSAM are removed and may be reported to law enforcement. We do not allow minors to use Smeet (see Section 5).

2. Our commitment to CSAM prevention laws

Smeet is committed to preventing CSAM and complying with applicable laws in Australia and internationally, including laws that require platforms to address illegal child abuse content and to cooperate with authorities. We review reports promptly, act on confirmed violations, and improve our safety tools over time.

3. In-app reporting

Every user can report another user or concerning content inside the Smeet app:

  • Open the other user’s profile or the relevant screen (for example a chat message).
  • Tap Report and choose a reason (such as harassment, inappropriate content, or other safety concerns).
  • Submit the report. It is stored securely so our team can review it.

Reports about CSAE, CSAM, or a child at risk are treated as highest priority. If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services first (in Australia dial 000), then report in the app and email child-safety@smeet.com.au.

4. In-app blocking

You can block another user at any time from their profile or related screens. When you block someone:

  • They should no longer be able to interact with you through match, chat, or discovery features as designed;
  • You can focus on your experience without further contact from that person.

Blocking does not replace reporting. If you see CSAE, CSAM, or serious harm, please report as well so we can take action on the account.

5. Adults only (18+)

Smeet is intended for an 18+ audience only. We do not knowingly allow children or teens under 18 to create accounts or use the Services. By signing up, users represent that they are at least 18 years old.

If we learn that a user is under 18, we will suspend or terminate the account and delete associated data where appropriate. Parents or guardians who believe a minor is using Smeet should contact child-safety@smeet.com.au immediately.

See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

6. Cooperation with law enforcement

Smeet will cooperate with law enforcement and authorised agencies when required by law or when necessary to protect children. This may include responding to valid legal requests and, where applicable, reporting to:

  • Australian Federal Police (AFP) and other Australian authorities;
  • The eSafety Commissioner under the Online Safety Act 2021 (Cth), where relevant;
  • NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children) or equivalent international bodies when cross-border CSAM is involved.

We preserve information as allowed by law to support investigations.

8. How we detect and remove CSAM

Smeet uses a combination of user tools and operator review:

MethodWhat we do
User reportsIn-app Report flow; reports reviewed by our team; CSAM/CSAE escalated immediately.
User blockingLimits further interaction while we investigate reports.
Server-side moderationReview of reported content and accounts; automated CSAM detection is planned as we scale.
Account enforcementWarning, suspension, or permanent ban; removal of violating content where technically feasible.
Law enforcementReferral when required or appropriate (Section 6).

We do not allow CSAM on our platform. Confirmed CSAM is removed and associated accounts are terminated.

9. Regional and international compliance

Australia

We align our practices with Australian law, including:

  • Online Safety Act 2021 (Cth) — including expectations for social platforms to address illegal and harmful material;
  • Criminal Code Act 1995 (Cth) — offences relating to child abuse material and exploitation.

International

We respect child protection norms reflected in the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child and cooperate with authorities in other jurisdictions when CSAM or CSAE crosses borders.

10. Updates to this page

We may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time. When we do, we will change the Last updated date at the top of this page. Material changes may be communicated in the app or by other reasonable means where appropriate.

Continued use of Smeet after an update means you accept the revised standards, subject to applicable law.
